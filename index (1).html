About me

My Story

I'm a Boston-based engineering student working toward my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering at Boston University, with a focus on computational modeling, biological systems, and engineering design.

I got an early start in STEM through dual enrollment at UMass Lowell and Middlesex Community College, which led to a research internship in UMass Lowell's chemistry labs. There I synthesized fluorescent nanoparticles for bio-imaging and helped develop and refine ELISA assays aimed at improving HIV detection accuracy.

At ZOLL Medical, I moved from the lab to the production floor, assembling medical devices in a regulated FDA / ISO 13485 / GMP environment and taking part in quality audits and root cause analysis. Seeing how a device goes from design to a finished, tested product is what pointed me toward medical device product engineering.

Outside the classroom I'm a Resident Assistant at BU, a media technician at my church, and I train about 30 hours a week at Back to Basics MMA. The discipline from the mat carries straight into the lab.
